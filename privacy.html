Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how PublishFlow ("we," "us") handles data when you connect a TikTok account to the PublishFlow node inside n8n. It applies to anyone who authorizes PublishFlow to access their TikTok account.

2. Data we access

When you authorize PublishFlow, TikTok grants it access to:

  • Basic profile information for the connected account (display name and TikTok user ID), so you can confirm which account a workflow is posting to.
  • The ability to publish videos you supply through your own n8n workflow to that account.
  • Publish status and performance metrics for videos published through PublishFlow, so your workflow can report on them.

We do not access your existing TikTok content library, your followers, direct messages, or any account beyond the one you explicitly connect.

3. How we use it

Data passed through PublishFlow is used only to carry out the workflow step you configured: uploading the video you provided, checking its status, and returning that status and any metrics to your workflow. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the workflow you built.

4. Where data is stored

PublishFlow does not operate a server that stores your video files, captions, or TikTok data after a workflow run finishes. Your TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored as an encrypted credential inside your own n8n instance — self-hosted by you, or hosted by n8n Cloud, depending on how you run n8n. Any long-term storage of publish results (in a database, spreadsheet, or log) happens only because your own workflow was built to write it there, and is under your control.

5. Sharing

We do not sell data or share it with third parties. Data flows only between TikTok's API and the n8n instance you run — there is no PublishFlow-operated intermediary handling your data in transit.

6. Retention

We retain nothing beyond the lifetime of a single workflow execution, since we do not operate a backend that stores your data. Your n8n instance retains execution logs according to your own n8n retention settings, which you control.

7. Your controls

  • Revoke PublishFlow's access at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings in your TikTok account.
  • Delete the stored credential from your n8n instance to remove the locally stored tokens.
  • Contact us to ask what data we hold or to request deletion of anything under our control.

8. Children's privacy

PublishFlow is a developer tool intended for use by adults building automation workflows, and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the project's changelog or repository.
